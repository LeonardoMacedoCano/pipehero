# Achievements

Achievements require being **logged in with Google** — see
[`SETTINGS.md`](./SETTINGS.md#account). Unlocking one pops up a toast
immediately, wherever you are in the app.

Open the **🏆 Achievements** page (same rail/drawer as Options) to see
all of them: a **X / Y unlocked** counter, an **Unlocked / Locked**
toggle, and — on unlocked ones — when you got it and what percentage
of all players have it too.

## Full list (18)

### Getting started

| | Achievement | How to unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 🎮 | Welcome to the Show | Log in with your Google account for the first time. |
| 🥁 | Getting Started | Finish your first song. |
| 🎨 | Personal Touch | Save a custom setting — theme, key bindings, strum mode, calibration, or graphics quality. |

### Skill & mastery

| | Achievement | How to unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 💯 | Perfect Run | Finish a song with no misses and no dropped sustains. |
| ⭐ | Five-Star Performance | Score 5 stars on any song. |
| 👑 | Expert Class | Score 5 stars on Expert difficulty. |
| ⚡ | First Boost | Complete a Star Power phrase without breaking it. |
| 🔥 | Never Broken | Finish a song without breaking a single Star Power phrase. |
| 💀 | Nerves of Steel | Finish a song after your Rock Meter dropped into the red critical zone. |
| 🔗 | On a Roll | Reach a 100-note combo in a single run. |
| 🚀 | Unstoppable | Reach a 300-note combo in a single run. |

### Collection

| | Achievement | How to unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 🎶 | Setlist Regular | Score results on 5 different songs. |
| 🥇 | Difficulty Master | Score results on all 4 difficulties of the same song. |
| 🏆 | Full Setlist | Score a result on every difficulty of every song currently in the library. |

### Special

| | Achievement | How to unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 💥 | That's Rock and Roll | Fail a song for the first time. |
| 🌙 | Night Owl | Finish a song between midnight and 4 AM. |

### Friends

| | Achievement | How to unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 🤝 | Squad Goals | Add your first friend. |
| ⚔️ | Friendly Rival | Win more song/difficulty comparisons than a friend in a head-to-head. |

Descriptions are shown up front even for achievements you haven't
unlocked yet — nothing here is a hidden mystery, just a checklist.
